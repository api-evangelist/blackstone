Blackstone explores how its investment teams evaluate opportunities across diverse industries, using examples from sandwich shops to space launches to artificial intelligence to illustrate that seemingly unrelated businesses can fit within the same investment framework. The piece features Viral Patel discussing the criteria and methodology Blackstone employs when assessing potential portfolio companies. The discussion highlights how Blackstone's playbook emphasizes scalable business models and the capacity for operational improvement regardless of industry.
